Valle de Guadahornillos, Parque Natural de las Sierras de Cazorla, Segura y Las Villas, Jaen, SE Spain. 1250 m.  This was our main study site for the work on Phillyrea latifolia seed dispersal. Besides, we are currently monitoring the long-term patterns of flowering and frutiing of Quercus ilex.  The two last photos are views of the Roblehondo field station (1300 m).
