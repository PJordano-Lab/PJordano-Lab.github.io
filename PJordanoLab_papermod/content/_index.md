Our research focuses on the study of biological diversity (biodiversity) from both ecological and evolutionary perspectives. We are interested in how ecological interactions, e.g. mutualisms, shape complex ecological systems. Interactions, such as those among plants and their seed dispersers and pollinators, are the wireframe of biodiversity, yet we are far from understanding how they evolve and coevolve. Our main research tools to address this fascinating theme include field ecology, molecular genetics, and theoretical ecology.
