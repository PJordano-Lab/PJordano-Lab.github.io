Song thrush (*Turdus philomelos*) eating holly berries (*Ilex aquifolium*) and blackcap (*Sylvia atricapilla*) eating lentisc fruits (*Pistacia lentiscus*). The long fruiting period of hollies and lentiscs, from autumn to late winter, overlaps with the northward migration, therefore, song thrushes and blackcaps have the potential to disperse holly and lentisc seeds at long distances towards cooler latitudes (photos | left: David Chapman; right: Agustín Povedano)
