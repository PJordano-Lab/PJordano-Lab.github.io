Nava de las Correhuelas field site

<<  Page 1 2  >>

This is the Nava de las Correhuelas field site, within Parque Natural de las Sierras de Cazorla, Segura y Las Villas, Jaén province, SE Spain. This was our main study site for the work on the seed dispersal ecology and genetics of Prunus mahaleb. We are currently monitoring the long-term patterns of flowering and frutiing of Prunus mahaleb and the dispersal ecology of Taxus baccata. Click on a picture to enlarge it.
