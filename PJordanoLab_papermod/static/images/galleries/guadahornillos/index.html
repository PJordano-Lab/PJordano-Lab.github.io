Guadahornillos Valley

This is the Guadahornillos valley within Parque Natural de las Sierras de Cazorla, Segura y Las Villas, Jaén province, SE Spain. This was our main study site for the work on Phillyrea latifolia seed dispersal. Besides, we are currently monitoring the long-term patterns of flowering and frutiing of Quercus ilex.
